    How to use

    1. 1

      Add pages. Scan with the camera: the page edges are outlined, tap the shutter for each page, then press Done. Or choose images, or drop them anywhere on the page.

    2. 2

      Check the crop. The rectangle shows what will be kept. Adjust it, then press Crop & add. Use full image keeps everything; Skip drops the photo.

    3. 3

      Arrange. Press and hold a page, then drag it to a new place. Tap a page to preview it and switch between cropped and original. The bin removes it.

    4. 4

      Name it and download. Type a file name, then press Download PDF. Use Add image to put more pages in the same PDF.
